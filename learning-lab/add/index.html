CSDE IEP & 504 Supplementary Aids, Accommodations & Modifications

Reference based on Connecticut State Department of Education (CSDE) Bureau of Special Education Framework.

1. Accommodations

Category Accommodation Examples Implementation Guidelines
Presentation Large Print Text, Orally Read Tests/Directions, Highlighted or Color-Coded Texts, Pair Written and Oral Instruction, Repeat/Rephrase Instructions, Simplify Test Wording, Supplementary Visuals, Speech to Text Devices. Provide alternative formats or visual supports to facilitate content understanding without altering performance expectations.
Student Response Math Manipulatives, No Handwriting Penalty, Oral Testing, Speech to Text, Templates for Written Work, Use of Calculator, Word Prediction / Voice Recognition. Allow alternative modes for students to express knowledge and complete coursework.
Environment Adaptive Workspace, Clear Work Area, Preferential Seating, Reduction of Auditory/Visual Stimulation, Study Carrel, Use of Fidget Toys, Appropriate Lighting. Adjust classroom layout or physical setup to optimize student focus and comfort.
Time / Scheduling Break Between Tasks, Extra Time (Tests/Projects/Written Work/Assignments), Short Breaks Throughout the Day, Pace Long-Term Projects, Use Timer, Prior Notice of Tests. Provide flexible scheduling or time extensions to manage processing speed and endurance.
Organization Assignment Pad, Color-Coding, Daily Homework/Assignment Lists, Desktop List of Tasks, Electronic Organizers, Folders to Hold Work, Provide Agenda, Visual Schedule, Post Class Rules/Routines. Structure tasks, schedules, and materials to assist with executive dysfunction.
Behavioral Interventions Acknowledge Positive Work Frequently, Behavior Contracts, Cue Expected Behavior, De-escalation Strategies, Immediate Feedback, Peer Supports/Mentoring, Positive Reinforcement, Proximity Control. Utilize proactive behavioral strategies and immediate reinforcement to maintain engagement.
Instructional Strategies Check for Understanding, Concrete Examples, Extra Drill/Practice, Hands-on Projects, Multi-Sensory Approach, Pre-Teach Content, Provide Notes/Outlines, Vocabulary Word Banks. Employ differentiated instructional techniques to support learning retention.

2. Modifications

Category Modification Examples Implementation Guidelines
Content & Instruction Modified texts/reading material, Modified content for lessons/homework, Modified curriculum, Simplified Assignments. Alter learning expectations or target goals to match the student's individualized capabilities.
Performance Criteria Modified tests/assessments, Pass/Fail grading criteria, Audit Course options, Reduced answer choices. Adjust assessment criteria, grading scales, or response requirements.

Emergency De-escalation Protocol

  1. Maintain Safety: Clear immediate area if physical safety is compromised.
  2. Low-Arousal Approach: Use a calm tone, reduce verbal demands.
  3. Contact Crisis Team: Call Main Office (Ext. 0) or Learning Lab.
